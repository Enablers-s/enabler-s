  1. 01

    Company formation

    UAE & Singapore

    Bizabler-S

    Dual-hub entry without a banking dead end

    Challenge
    An earlier UAE licence used the wrong activity codes, so no Tier-1 bank would open an account.
    What we did
    Re-mapped the activities to the real business model and set up the UAE and Singapore entities in parallel, with a bank-ready file.

    ~8 weeks

    to licensed, banked and compliant in two countries

    Financial & tax

    UK · EU · GCC

    Finablers

    Cross-border tax structure rebuilt

    Anglo-Gulf industrial group · multi-entity

    Challenge
    A decade of ad-hoc structuring had drifted out of line with substance rules, with audit season approaching.
    What we did
